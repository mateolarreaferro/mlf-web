Generative agent-based modeling: societies of language-model agents you can run forward to rehearse a decision inside a simulated population before making it in the real one. The first Attractor, before the audio world models.

## notes

This is the GABM work (generative agent-based modeling) at attractor.live,
which the site used to link as "GABM" from the bio. It is Attractor Labs'
first product and the origin of the company; the company has since moved to
audio world models (attractor.world), and that current work is deliberately
NOT a node in the graph: the bio covers it, and Mateo asked that "Attractor"
itself not appear there. This node is the v1 research and product only.

Lineage: grew out of [[love-simulations]] and [[icebreaker]], both generative
agent simulations of social situations; v1 generalised that into a modeling
tool for complex social systems. Mateo is founder and CEO of the company.
