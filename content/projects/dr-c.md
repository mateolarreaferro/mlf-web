Dr.C is a domain-specialized AI collaborator for Csound, built for people learning to make sound as much as for people shipping it. We believe a generative tool should widen the space someone is working in rather than close it: instead of returning one finished file, Dr.C proposes a few working alternatives, renders the one that gets chosen, and keeps every version in a non-destructive history, so trying a second idea is never a step backward.

It draws on four decades of curated Csound material: the Csound Book, the community's journals, catalogs, and instrument collections. It also narrates the history behind a technique as it teaches it, so a lesson in DSP is also a lesson in where the field came from. It ships as two tools: a terminal application for production work, and a desktop application built for the classroom.
