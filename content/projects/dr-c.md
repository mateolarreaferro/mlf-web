Dr.C is a domain-specialized AI collaborator for Csound, built for people learning to make sound as much as for people shipping it. We believe a generative tool should widen the space someone is working in rather than close it: instead of returning one finished file, Dr.C proposes a few working alternatives, renders the one that gets chosen, and keeps every version in a non-destructive history. It draws on four decades of curated Csound material, and narrates the history behind a technique as it teaches it, so a lesson in DSP is also a lesson in where the field came from.
