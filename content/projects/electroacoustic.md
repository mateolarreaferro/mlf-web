## notes

Mateo's electroacoustic and computer-music composition, as distinct from the
songs he releases as [[juancho-lagartos]]. Known pieces: The13thRabbit,
Action Potential I, Arbol Infinito. He has also performed algorithmic sets
with Isla Saturno & Soios and with Aya Yuasa / Sam Wells (SPLICE Ensemble),
and keeps an ambient collection (the "ambiente" SoundCloud playlist, written up
as thought #1, "Un Disco Ambiental para Terminar el Día").
