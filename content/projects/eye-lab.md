## notes

Repo: github.com/mateolarreaferro/Eye-Lab. Vision games, tests and spatial-frequency filters
(high-pass "frequency patching" and low-pass) for the Sinha lab's Project Prakash, which treats
children born blind and studies how the brain learns to see. It began as a Godot app for macOS and
became a web app (the repo's web/ folder) in October 2026 for the lab to play with. A small macOS
helper filters the whole screen. Iris, a Claude-powered guide, answers questions about the games and
the research using each visitor's own API key. It is a research and training tool, not a medical
device.
