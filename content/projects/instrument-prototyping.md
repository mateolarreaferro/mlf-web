**Vibe Coding of Sound Artifacts** — "In Stanford's 220A: Computer-Generated
Sound, we explored the creation of musical artifacts, including several data
sonification tools, through real-time, LLM-assisted workflows implemented
entirely with Web Audio technologies. Under the guidance of Dr. Chris Chafe at
CCRMA, the course is structured around two weekly exercises: first, a
traditionally hand-coded music software application; and second, an
LLM-generated counterpart, where the emphasis shifts from technical
implementation to creative ideation and system design."
Repo: github.com/mateolarreaferro/VibeCoding-Audio-Artifacts
