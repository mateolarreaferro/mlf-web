A simulation tool for exploring how people think, feel, and adapt within everyday relationship scenarios. Rather than optimizing for perfect matches, LoveSims uses simulated interactions and structured self-reflection to help users examine their responses and perspectives. By modeling the complexity of real-life dynamics, we aim to support a deeper understanding of ourselves and our patterns of connection.
