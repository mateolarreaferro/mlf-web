A breathing interface to prevent screen apnea: a circle that breathes at the edge of your attention while you work, in as it grows and out as it shrinks. Set how deep the breath goes, and the pads add an ambience (noise, café, forest, drone) or a sound at each turn. Breathe with it here, or keep it in a corner of your screen.

---

## notes

Made in fall 2024 for Artful Design (Stanford, Music 256A) in ChucK and
ChuGL (Periphery.ck). In October 2026 it was ported to play in its card
(see CLAUDE.md, "Periphery"), with a "keep it in a corner" pop-out window and
a browser extension that keeps the breath in the corner of every page.

The old site's captions: "in the background: using it while making a
sketch" and "on the side: using it while cleaning EEG data", which is
exactly what the pop-out and the extension are for.

The card description is mine, written from the code; Mateo may reword it.
