At Prisms of Reality I worked as a Unity developer on a large-scale VR education platform for math and science learning, responsible for designing and standardizing the platform's multimodal interaction grammars across audio, haptics, and visuals. These grammars defined how mathematical and scientific concepts were mapped to perceptual feedback, keeping lessons consistent and legible across modules. I also built the platform's original localization pipeline and contributed to subtitle and speech technologies, and served as principal engineer for the Rate of Change module, used by roughly one million K-12 students.
