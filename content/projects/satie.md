A creativity-support tool for generative spatial audio. Satie is a human-in-the-loop generative interface that preserves creative intention while reducing technical burden — structured, interpretable layers like domain-specific languages, spatial abstractions, and gesture-based controls let composers shape timing, variation, and behavior without surrendering authorship to automation.
