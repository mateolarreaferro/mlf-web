A Post-it style wall for any friend who visits: an infinite canvas where notes appear for everyone in real time. Double-click anywhere to leave one, signed with your name or anonymous, in English or Spanish. Anyone can reply or react, and only you can change or delete your own notes. It is the same premise as the private wall shared with Marielisa, opened up to everyone who passes by.

## notes

Sticky Notes is the open version of Ansantuario, the private Post-it app
Mateo built for Marielisa: an infinite wall where their notes appear on each
other's computers in real time, with a daily prompt in Spanish, text or voice
notes, and a memory agent that searches past notes by phrase, keyword, or
meaning and suggests new prompts from recurring themes. The "watch video"
link shows that private app, and the repository holds both versions.

The open wall runs on this site at mateolarreaferro.com/sticky-notes (the
"interactive demo" button). Anyone can leave a text note, signed with a name
or anonymous, and see everyone else's appear. It is in English or Spanish,
the visitor's choice, and opens on a splash with the instructions. It is a
separate wall from Mateo and Marielisa's private one, which stays private, and
it has none of the private app's AI features: no prompts, no memory search,
no voice notes.
