Synchronicity is a biofeedback-driven audiovisual system that turns physiological signals into a live graphical score for performers to interpret. It maps blood pressure to color as a marker of harmonic density, heart-rate variability to pulsation as an environmental metronome, and galvanic skin response to fog density as an indicator of dissonance threshold. Audience members contribute these signals in real time, so their internal states shape the evolving performance. The piece externalizes subtle internal fluctuations into structures a performer can read, turning the audience into active collaborators.
