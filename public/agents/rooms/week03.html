Melody: arranging with DrC

Reflection

What worked

The interface is convenient for a musician trying to get a melodic or progression idea across without necessarily having the right equipment to record it (e.g. a keyboard). It lets you do it from the computer keyboard and then have the AI transform it into something more polished.

In this case it's also about synthesis. We spend so much time trying to get to the right patch to then play a piece. Here it's easy to describe in natural language the type of instrument or synth sound I want, and even a bit of the DSP, so that I can focus more on the composition and less on setting up the patch.

Where it struggled

It's hard to describe sound using words (sometimes). It should be able to understand my own language: for instance, "less cheesy", and understand from the progression or melody what makes it cheesy. It's not great at that yet.

What should improve

The interface needs some cleaning up so that I can export what I produce into a MIDI file, or record it as a stem that I can then use in my DAW. And a better language schema (see above).

What it says about the objective

I see this as a pure human-AI co-creation interface. It's not about delegating a task, it's about engaging in a process together, which I find more interesting.
